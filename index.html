About Me

I am currently pursuing an M.Tech in Product Design & Management at IIIT Hyderabad. Before this, I completed my B.S. in Data Science & Applications from IIT Madras.

My academic background is primarily in data, technology, programming, machine learning, software development, data analysis, and business analytics. I enjoy coding and solving problems — but over time, I became interested not only in how a technical solution is built, but also in questions like:

  • Who are we building for?
  • What problem are we actually solving?
  • What makes a product useful?
  • How can technology and design work together?

This curiosity is what led me toward Product Design & Management. I'm currently exploring the intersection of technology, data, product design, and people.
